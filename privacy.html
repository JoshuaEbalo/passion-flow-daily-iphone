Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Passion Flow Daily ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Passion Flow Daily mobile application and website.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication).
  • User-Generated Content: Journal entries, mood logs, flow activities, and moments you create within the app are stored in your personal account.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized data about how you use the app to improve our service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To sync your data across devices via your account
  • To send account-related communications (e.g., password reset)
  • To improve the app experience

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). Firebase complies with industry-standard security practices. Your journal entries and personal data are private to your account and are not shared with other users.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and data storage
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions (we do not store your payment details)

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app's Settings screen, which will permanently remove all your data.

Children's Privacy

Passion Flow Daily is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request data deletion, you can delete your account in the app or contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
